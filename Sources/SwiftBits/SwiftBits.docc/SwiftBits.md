# ``SwiftBits``

Drop-in motion and visual flair for SwiftUI.

## Overview

SwiftBits provides focused building blocks for the expressive parts of an
interface — animated text, tactile buttons, and eye-catching surfaces — plus a
handful of everyday components. Everything renders and animates natively, ships
with sensible defaults and `LocalizedStringKey` labels, and respects Reduce
Motion, Reduce Transparency, Dynamic Type, and VoiceOver. It is inspired by
[React Bits](https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits), rebuilt for SwiftUI.
